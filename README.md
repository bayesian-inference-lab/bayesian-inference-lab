Welcome to the <b>B</b>ayesian <b>I</b>nference <b>L</b>ab (<b>BIL</b>) at Inha University.  
We pursue fundamental methodology and applied research at the intersection of Bayesian statistics, machine learning, and deep learning.

## Research Focus

- **Bayesian Inference & Probabilistic Modeling**  
  Principled probabilistic frameworks and efficient approximate inference algorithms.

- **Statistical Machine & Deep Learning**  
  Synergy between statistical theory and scalable machine/deep learning architectures.

- **Uncertainty Quantification & Robustness**  
  Predictive uncertainty estimation and robust inference under model misspecification.

- **Applied Probabilistic Intelligence**  
  Probabilistic modeling applications for complex real-world statistical data science problems.
